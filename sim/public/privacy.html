Privacy policy

Effective November 16, 2026

archy for iPhone does not collect any data about you. Everything you enter or scan stays on your iPhone. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking.

What the app stores on your iPhone

  • Your answers to the welcome questions, such as your goal and any foot pain you report.
  • Foot measurements you enter and the results worked out from them.
  • Foot scans: the photos taken during a scan and the 3D model built from them.
  • Insole requests you save, and your app settings.

This is kept in the app's own storage on your device. We cannot see it, and the app does not send it to us or to anyone else.

Camera

The app uses the camera and LiDAR sensor only while you scan a foot. The photos are processed on your iPhone to build the 3D model. They are not uploaded.

Sharing

Nothing leaves your iPhone unless you choose to share it. If you tap Share on a scan or an insole request, iOS sends that file to the app or person you pick. What happens next is covered by their privacy policy.

Links

The research list links to outside websites such as nasa.gov. They open in your browser and have their own privacy policies.

Deleting your data

You can delete scans and saved insole requests inside the app. Deleting the app removes everything it stored, including your answers and results.

Children

archy is not directed at children under 13, and it collects no data from anyone.

This website

The web demo at tryarchy.vercel.app runs in your browser and does not upload your files. The site is hosted by Vercel, which keeps standard server logs such as IP addresses.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
