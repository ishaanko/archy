Help with archy for iPhone

Email narsipurpranav@gmail.com and we'll get back to you. Please include your iPhone model and iOS version.

Which iPhones can scan?

Scanning needs an iPhone with a LiDAR sensor: iPhone 12 Pro or later Pro and Pro Max models. On other iPhones you can still measure by hand, view the sample scan, design an insole and use the foot exercises.

My scan failed or looks wrong

  • Scan in a bright, evenly lit room.
  • Keep the foot still and move the phone slowly all the way around it.
  • Make sure your iPhone has a few gigabytes of free space.
  • Open How to scan on the Scan tab for a walkthrough.

Is archy a medical app?

No. archy is a screening and education tool. Its measurements are not clinically validated and it does not diagnose anything. If you have foot pain or a health concern, see a clinician.

Does an insole request order anything?

No. A request is a file you can share with a maker to ask for a quote. Nothing is ordered or charged in the app.

Where is my data?

On your iPhone only. See the privacy policy.

How do I delete my data?

Delete scans and saved insole requests inside the app. Deleting the app removes everything, including your answers and results.

archy is a screening and education tool, not a medical device. It does not diagnose or treat any condition.
